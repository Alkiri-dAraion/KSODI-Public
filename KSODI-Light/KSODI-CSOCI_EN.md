# KSODI English Translation Table

## Naming boundary

**KSODI is the sole name of the method in German and English.**

`CSOCI` is only a translation mnemonic formed from the initial letters of the
English KSODI-Light labels: **C**ontext, **S**tructure, **O**bjectivity,
**C**larity and **I**nformation Depth. It may be used in a translation table to
help readers map the German source terms to English. It is not an alternative
method name.

CSOCI has no separate method definition, version line, governance model,
authorship, repository lineage or licence. It must not be presented or cited as
an independently originated English method or as a separate intellectual
property line.

Creating or using an English translation does not transfer ownership of the
underlying KSODI material. An adapter may hold rights in their own original
contributions, but use of the underlying KSODI material remains subject to
[CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE.md): attribution must be retained, modifications must be
identified and no endorsement or official status may be implied.

## Translation mapping

| KSODI letter | German source term | English KSODI-Light label | English mnemonic letter | Observer-facing v3.5 label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K | Kontext | Context | C | Observable Context Completeness |
| S | Struktur | Structure | S | Observable Structural Coherence |
| O | Objektivität | Objectivity | O | Observable Grounded Objectivity |
| D | Deutlichkeit | Clarity | C | Observable Clarity |
| I | Informationsgehalt | Information Depth | I | Observable Information Impulse |

Read vertically, the optional English mnemonic column produces `CSOCI`. The
method name nevertheless remains `KSODI`.

The English labels are functional translations, not claims that the Light and
Observer layers use identical wording. KSODI-Light uses accessible labels for
reflection, training and prompt guidance. Standard-Eval and KSODI-Full use the
more precise observer-facing reconstruction labels shown in the final column.

## Recommended reference

In English text, use **KSODI**. Where the mnemonic is helpful, introduce it only
as a mapping, for example:

> KSODI (English translation mnemonic: CSOCI)

Do not use `CSOCI` alone as the name of the method.

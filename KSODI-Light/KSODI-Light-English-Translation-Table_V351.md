# KSODI English Translation Table v3.51

## 1. Naming boundary

**KSODI is the sole name of the method in German and English.**

`CSOCI` is only a translation mnemonic formed from the initial letters of the
accessible English KSODI-Light labels: **C**ontext, **S**tructure,
**O**bjectivity/Grounding, **C**larity and **I**nformation/Impulse. It is not an
alternative method name.

CSOCI has no separate method definition, version line, governance model,
authorship, repository lineage or licence. It must not be presented or cited
as an independently originated English method or separate intellectual-
property line.

Creating or using an English translation does not transfer ownership of the
underlying KSODI material. An adapter may hold rights in their own original
contributions, but use of the underlying KSODI material remains subject to
[CC BY 4.0](./LICENSE.md): attribution must be retained, modifications must be
identified and no endorsement or official status may be implied.

## 2. Translation mapping

| KSODI letter | German source/orientation term | Accessible English KSODI-Light label | English mnemonic letter | Formal Observer-facing label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K | Kontext | Context | C | Observable Context Completeness |
| S | Struktur | Structure | S | Observable Structural Coherence |
| O | Objektivierbarkeit / Grundierung | Objectivity / Grounding | O | Observable Grounded Objectivity |
| D | Unterscheidbarkeit / Deutlichkeit | Clarity / Distinguishability | C | Observable Clarity |
| I | Informationsgehalt / Impuls | Information / Impulse | I | Observable Information Impulse |

Read vertically, the optional English mnemonic column produces `CSOCI`. The
method name nevertheless remains `KSODI`.

The accessible labels support reflection, teaching and prompt guidance. The
formal Observer labels name versioned reconstruction and measurement
dimensions. The two vocabularies remain related, but they are not
interchangeable contracts.

## 3. Value-domain boundary

This file translates terms; it does not convert values.

| Surface | Possible value form | Meaning boundary |
|---|---|---|
| KSODI-Light | natural-language feedback; optionally a rough internal `0..5` estimate per task-relevant dimension | task-relative orientation or completeness estimate; not a formal operator result and not a person rating |
| Layer-1 Observer | typed numeric value in the applicable `[0,1]` domain or a nonnumeric status under the exact operator contract | formal, profile-bound operator result |
| Later views | typed states, projections, gates or relational results under their own contracts | never inferred by rescaling a Light estimate |

There is no canonical transformation from a Light `0..5` estimate to a formal
Layer-1 value. Do not sum the five Light estimates into a preferred total or
use such a total as a substitute for complete dimensional feedback.

## 4. Recommended reference

In English text, use **KSODI**. Where the mnemonic is useful, introduce it only
as a mapping, for example:

> KSODI (English translation mnemonic: CSOCI)

Do not use `CSOCI` alone as the name of the method.

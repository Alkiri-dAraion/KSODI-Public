# KSODI Framework and Protocol — v3.51 Naming Transition

Status: `PUBLIC_DECISION_NOTE / V351 / NON_METHOD_AUTHORITY /
PROTOCOL_EFFECT_UNVALIDATED`

## Decision

Current KSODI work uses **KSODI Framework** for the connective orientation
across Project, Method, roles, evidence handoffs, implementations and
separately governed action surfaces. **KSODI Protocol** names a
still-exploratory interaction and repair practice.

Earlier publications used:

- **IDAS — Interactive Dialogue, Analytics & Steering** for the broader
  framework idea; and
- **SIRA** for a lightweight interaction-practice protocol.

The naming transition does not discard that work. It reduces acronym load,
keeps the established KSODI name central and avoids allowing the title words
`Steering`, hidden `Intention` or overloaded `Resonance` to imply claims that
the current method does not make.

## What remains

The following concerns continue under KSODI names:

- distinction among Project, Method, working mode, implementation and action;
- roles, spaces, convention delivery, evidence handoffs and provenance;
- reflective exploration and bounded production;
- visible inquiry, response, repair and revision;
- navigable interaction views with return paths to evidence;
- strict separation of Observer findings from policy, Controller and action.

## Authority boundary

The versioned KSODI Method files remain authoritative for calculations,
profiles, statuses and interpretation limits. The KSODI Framework does not add
a formula. The KSODI Protocol remains a candidate practice and is not a
mandatory Method stage, validated effect or action authority.

The KSODI Framework is not identical with the KSODI Architecture: Architecture
maps the topology of Method objects and layers, while Framework relates the
wider project, working and authority surfaces without merging them.

## Project and organization

**KSODI Project** names the wider research, method-development,
implementation, validation, governance and publication programme.
`KSODI-project` is the related GitHub organization and repository umbrella.
The organization is a hosting and coordination surface, not a Method version,
formula, validation state or transfer authority.

`KSODI` may serve as a short form where the scope is unambiguous. Statements
whose meaning depends on scope, version, role, intended use or authority should
qualify whether they concern the Project, Method, Framework, Protocol, one
method scope, one implementation or one publication snapshot.

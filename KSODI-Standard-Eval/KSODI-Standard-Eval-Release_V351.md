# KSODI Standard-Eval v3.51

This folder contains the public v3.51 Standard-Eval Observer structure for
source-attributed monadic observation.

The public scope contains:

- Layer 1: the five related but non-interchangeable operator families
  `K`, `S`, `O`, `D` and `I`, their declared profiles and conditional
  implementation companions;
- Layer 2: complete typed `Z`, partial views, source-local dynamics and derived
  window objects;
- Layer 3: the monadic IK family with `IK_w`, `IK_M2`, `IK_ref`,
  `AxisDev_ref`, reduced `IK_w^[M]`, dynamics and window-order contracts.

The human-readable `IK-Gesamtbild` and technical
`ik_joint_evaluation_view` expose jointly navigable readings without creating
one total score. Purpose-required machine objects, assistive interpretations
and human/governance displays remain distinguishable and reconstructible to
their admissible operands and provenance.

After typed Z, monadic IK and the KSODI-Full `R0` gate are separate branches.
Standard-Eval does not define relation, resonance, coupling or Controller
authority. Current dyadic `IK_rel` belongs to KSODI-Full and retains its own
pairing and exact open-R0 handoff.

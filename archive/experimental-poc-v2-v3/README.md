# Experimental PoC Ideas from the KSODI V2/V3 Context

This folder preserves two historical KSODI/EDEN Proof-of-Concept idea papers.
Their underlying ideas arose during the V2/V3 exploration period, before the
private repository was established as the current method workbench. The files
were later added to and, in one case, editorially revised in the public
repository.

They document an exploratory attempt to connect an early KSODI-Light scoring
approach with an EDEN maturity model. They are development history, not current
KSODI v3.50 method, implementation, validation, compliance or deployment
guidance.

In particular, the papers retain:

- the earlier Light scale direction, which is inverse to the current scale;
- proposed thresholds and an experimental KSODI-to-EDEN mapping;
- provisional claims about measurability, readiness, training effects,
  efficiency, compliance, privacy, scalability and trust;
- an external in-context-learning citation that does not validate KSODI or the
  proposed mapping.

These historical hypotheses do not establish that the current method is
unsound. They show an earlier exploration whose assumptions were not carried
forward as current v3.50 claims. The archived files retain their original
content and licence wording; archive placement classifies methodological and
version status, not legal interpretation.

# KSODI Public Archive

This is the single archive area for historical KSODI material in this
repository. Archived files preserve provenance, earlier publication states and
the development history of the method. They are not current v3.5 method
specifications or implementation guidance.

## Archive Areas

- [`legacy-cc-by-4.0-till-2025/`](./legacy-cc-by-4.0-till-2025/README.md) —
  earlier published material retained under its historical licence boundary;
- [`document-snapshots/`](./document-snapshots/) — superseded states of public
  orientation and method documents;
- [`method-history-v33/`](./method-history-v33/README.md) — historical v3.3
  Standard-Eval and transition material;
- [`experimental-poc-v2-v3/`](./experimental-poc-v2-v3/README.md) — historical
  KSODI/EDEN PoC ideas from the V2/V3 exploration context, retained as
  development history rather than current v3.50 guidance;
- [`assets-archive/historical-observer-v342/`](./assets-archive/historical-observer-v342/README.md)
  — historically important Observer images from the v3.3 implementation and
  v3.42-to-v3.5 transition context.

For the current method, begin at the repository root [`README.md`](../README.md).

## KSODI Method · Framework · Protocol

---

## Purpose of this document

This document defines the **ethical boundaries, intended use and non-use** of
the KSODI Method, the wider KSODI Framework and the still-exploratory KSODI
Protocol.

It is not a legal policy.
It is an **explicit statement of responsibility** regarding observation and
evaluation of interaction between distinguishable entities and any separately
governed later use in stabilization processes—especially human–AI,
human-agent and agentic contexts involving **autonomous or semi-autonomous
agents**.

---

## Core ethical position

KSODI-Standard-Eval and KSODI-Full are **Observer-oriented method scopes**,
not control systems. KSODI-Light is a reflective working layer and does not
perform formal Observer calculations. The KSODI Framework and candidate
Protocol may frame separately governed transitions from observation toward
possible action, but neither a KSODI finding nor a Framework/Protocol relation
authorizes action by itself.

They are designed to:

* make interaction behavior visible,
* make declared indicators of possible instability and drift visible over
  time,
* support human understanding and governance decisions.

They are **not** designed to:

* automate decisions,
* enforce behavior,
* judge people,
* or replace human responsibility.

## Method and observation boundaries

The ethical boundary follows the method architecture:

* **KSODI-Standard-Eval** observes one source-attributed monadic trajectory
  through `K/S/O/D/I`, `Z_A(k)` and, where selected, `IK_A(k)`.
* **KSODI-Full** begins with dyadic or explicitly declared n-adic observation
  at `R_0`. The contributing trajectories remain distinguishable.
* An open numeric canonical complete `R_0` gate under the exact required
  profile permits only a compatible defined relational observation; it does
  not prove acknowledgement, shared meaning, coupling, intent or causality.
* An **Observer** reconstructs and reports observable findings. A Controller,
  intervention or other action requires a separate architecture, authority,
  policy and audit trail.

Shared context, interaction or infrastructure must never be used to silently
merge distinct people, agents, events or trajectories into one observed
entity.

Observation granularity must also be declared and reviewable. Treating a
person, subsystem, team or composite performance as the evaluation unit can
change what a result means and which comparisons are valid. Part-whole and
group mappings must remain recoverable; the same event must not be counted at
several levels without disclosure, and subsystem observation must not be
misrepresented as a judgement about the whole person or entity.

### Cross-layer and Micro–Macro return path

The ethical contract must remain reconstructible through every observation
resolution:

```text
human-facing question or view
-> declared operator evidence and typed result
-> complete attributable Z or explicit non-complete assembly
-> selected monadic or relational derivation
-> exact identities, profiles, windows, provenance and exclusions
```

A readable projection is not a simplified replacement truth. Every material
display, alert, ranking candidate or aggregate must retain a practical return
path to the machine-readable object and the evidence limits that produced it.
Conversely, technical richness does not remove the duty to provide an
understandable view to an affected or responsible human.

The Micro view must not silently erase the wider context, purpose or affected
population. The Macro view must not erase its members, exclusions, sampling,
uncertainty or source-local histories. A group, team, window or point cloud is
a separately identified derived observation object; it must not be written
back as the state or quality of one participant.

### Layer 0 and formal observation

KSODI-Light supports reflection, clarification and local self-adjustment under
disclosed prompt-level guidance. It does not calculate formal observer results
and must not be presented as a hidden personality, competence or quality
assessment. A person declining, misunderstanding or not sharing the Light
frame is not thereby deficient.

Nor does the five-part baseline imply that every practical question always
requires all five dimensions at equal relevance or weight. Which dimensions
are needed, foregrounded or deliberately left outside one bounded inquiry is
an open, context- and purpose-dependent methodological question. Any reduced
set or non-uniform weighting must be declared as a question/profile choice;
it must not be inferred from a person's disagreement, non-use or apparent
difficulty, and it does not silently change the current formal Standard-Eval
contracts.

Where a formal Observer is used, the applicable disclosure must distinguish
Light guidance from K/S/O/D/I measurement, storage, later relational analysis
and possible human or Controller action. A prompt-level working agreement does
not by itself constitute consent to every later data use or evaluation scope.

### Layer 1 through Layer 5 interpretation limits

K/S/O/D/I results describe one declared observable question about one
source-attributed event under one profile. They are not stable traits of a
person, agent, organization, language, culture or species. Grounding is not
truth; clarity is not agreement; structure is not correctness; information
impulse is not usefulness; context completeness is always relative to its
declared requirement and vantage.

Complete typed Z preserves the five coordinate readings without becoming a
psychological or ontological state. A non-complete Z assembly must remain
typed; missing or excluded evidence must never be imputed merely to enable a
scalar or comparison.

`IK_w`, `IK_M2`, `IK_ref` and `AxisDev_ref`, reviewed together with complete Z
as the non-scalar `IK-Gesamtbild`, answer different bounded questions. Their
combination is not a total score, person rank or replacement for complete Z.
The unrounded computational result, numeric policy, display value and rendered
mark remain distinguishable. Rounded or simplified displays must never become
later calculation operands.

An open R0 gate establishes only eligibility under its exact profile. It does
not establish relationship quality, shared meaning, shared direction,
alignment, safety or permission. Current IK_rel reports one bounded form of
dyadic movement compatibility after its exact gate; it does not establish
agreement, empathy, reciprocity, coupling, causation or successful
communication. Gate-closed or non-evaluable observations may remain important
for a different declared question and must not be discarded or moralized.

## Tools, not weapons

KSODI treats AI systems as **tools embedded in socio-technical contexts**.

Like many tools, AI can be used in different domains:

* education,
* administration,
* healthcare,
* infrastructure,
* research,
* and yes, potentially also defense-related contexts.

The ethical distinction does **not** lie in the tool itself,
but in **how it is used, observed and governed**.

A knife can be:

* a medical instrument,
* a kitchen tool,
* or a weapon.

KSODI does not decide *what* an AI system should do. It can help humans ask
whether observable behavior under a declared profile is changing in ways that
may matter to an independently defined purpose. That finding is not itself a
diagnosis of goal drift or hidden intent.

## Autonomous agents and responsibility

Autonomous or semi-autonomous agents pose specific ethical challenges:

* gradual goal drift,
* unintended task expansion,
* loss of contextual alignment,
* overconfidence without awareness,
* reduced human oversight over time.

The KSODI Method and wider Framework address these risks by:

* observing interaction structure over time,
* making possible observable drift indicators available for review,
* supporting human-in-the-loop oversight,
* and providing typed evidence to authorized human or governed decision
  processes.

No KSODI result guarantees early detection, prevention of harm or correct
intervention timing. Those outcomes require independent validation and
application-specific governance.

Responsibility always remains with:

* the designers,
* the operators,
* and the organizations deploying such systems.

## Observation over control

A central ethical principle of KSODI is:

> **Observation must never silently turn into control.**

The public v3.51
[Observability and Separate Controlled-Action Boundary](./KSODI-Full/KSODI_Observability-and-Controlled-Action-Boundary_V351.md)
records the corresponding authority map, evidence-envelope boundary and
anti-circularity guards without creating action authority.

Missing evidence must be typed, never scored. A result such as
`not_observable`, `not_applicable` or `not_selected` is not a numeric low value
and must not be converted into one. In particular, an unavailable observation
must never be reported as weak context, poor grounding or low quality of a
person, agent or source.

Therefore, ethically admissible use:

* must not deploy KSODI covertly,
* must not present inferred hidden intentions as observations,
* must not use KSODI for hidden profiling of individuals,
* and must not turn findings into action without declared authority and
  accountable governance.

Measurements should be:

* explicit,
* explainable,
* and based on observable interaction patterns only.

Evaluation profiles, selected axes and weights are governed choices rather
than neutral facts. They must be declared, versioned and retained with the
result. No implementation may silently reweight people, agents, trajectories
or comparison groups. A changed profile can change projections or rankings
without any change in the underlying observations; that change must remain
visible and reviewable.

### Separate consumers and assistive interpretation

The deterministic Observer path, an assistive evaluating model and a human
governance or domain-expert view are different consumers and evidence
channels. The Observer emits typed records. An assistive LLM may propose a
bounded semantic interpretation only from a declared evidence package and
must expose uncertainty, alternatives and claim limits. Its output remains an
attributable interpretation, not a method result or hidden-state finding.

Human decision makers need readable summaries and drill-down routes, not
manufactured certainty. Domain experts remain necessary wherever the meaning
of an observable pattern depends on clinical, pedagogical, communicative,
security, biological, linguistic or other specialist knowledge.

## Privacy and data minimization

Ethical AI governance requires restraint. KSODI does not require indiscriminate
access to all available content. The minimum required observation basis depends
on the declared operator, profile and application.

Depending on that basis, an Observer may need access to observable
contributions, declared context or reference material, source attribution and
technical metadata before it can construct numeric indicators, trajectories or
relational views. Raw language and reference material may contain personal,
confidential or otherwise sensitive information. Derived scores, embeddings,
vectors, trajectories and Hangar views are not automatically anonymous or
harmless merely because they no longer contain the original text.

Every implementation should therefore:

* collect only the data required for its declared observation purpose,
* keep raw material only where necessary and for a declared retention period,
* preserve provenance, access controls and deletion responsibilities,
* disclose what is observed and how findings may be used,
* keep observed data, Observer findings and later Controller actions distinct,
* and apply the relevant governance and legal requirements of its deployment
  context.

KSODI evaluates observable material. It does not provide direct access to
private thoughts, hidden internal states or unexposed model processes.

### Laboratory and productive retention

Laboratory and productive retention are different regimes:

- a controlled laboratory using synthetic, self-authored or explicitly
  admitted test material may retain raw carriers, bit patterns, language,
  intermediate states and derived results broadly enough to support
  reproducibility, reconstruction and later questions;
- productive use must select retention by declared purpose, necessity,
  affected rights, contractual and legal requirements, access risk and
  deletion responsibility. Raw language should be retained only as long as
  justified; derived vectors and calculations may have a different but still
  explicitly governed retention period.

Laboratory status is not a blanket exemption. If personal, confidential,
third-party or unexpectedly identifying material enters a test corpus, it must
be reclassified and governed accordingly.

### Windows, populations and partition preservation

Window and population summaries must declare exact membership, denominator,
coverage, overlap, time basis, aggregation order, profile and exclusions.
`not_observable`, `not_applicable`, gate-closed and processing partitions must
not be silently removed to produce a favorable numeric subset. A high result
over sparse eligible observations is not evidence of a sustained population or
relationship pattern.

Strict v3.51 aggregation and later partition-preserving bounded-tolerance
research answer different questions. Any later tolerance profile must keep its
excluded and failed partitions reconstructible, report sensitivity and avoid
turning missingness into zero. Macro indicators are navigation and diagnosis
openers; they are not automatic anomaly verdicts, individual labels or action
triggers.

## Defense, safety and sensitive domains

KSODI does not exclude sensitive domains by default.

However, its ethical use in such contexts requires:

* explicit human oversight,
* clear purpose limitation,
* transparent governance structures,
* and documented intervention thresholds.

Whether KSODI observations can support a particular **safety or stabilization**
process remains an application-specific research and validation question. A
KSODI value is not itself a safety finding, certification or permission to act,
and KSODI must never be used to legitimize autonomous harm.

### Dual-use boundary

KSODI is an observational method with dual-use potential. The same capacity to
make drift, instability or relational patterns visible can support responsible
safety work or be misused for surveillance, manipulation, targeting or the
optimization of coercive systems. A deployment must therefore declare its
purpose, affected entities, observation basis, authorized decision boundary
and means of human review before evaluation begins. KSODI results are evidence
under a declared profile; they are not permissions, target selections, truth
claims about a person or substitutes for accountable judgment. Findings must
not be silently repurposed across domains or converted into action merely
because they are numeric.

---

## Explicit non-use

The KSODI Method, Framework and candidate Protocol are **not compatible** with:

* autonomous lethal decision-making,
* covert behavioral manipulation,
* social scoring or hidden profiling,
* ideological enforcement,
* authoritarian control mechanisms,
* or systems designed to bypass human accountability.

Any such use contradicts the intent of the framework.

## Future Work remains open but separately governed

Ethical boundary-setting must not prematurely close legitimate research on
R-family relations, unknown signals, OCR or bit-pattern reconstruction,
security and forensic observation, assistive interpretation, Controller
architectures, training or new entity/domain types. Such work may preserve
several competing reconstructions and a `reconstruction_pending` state where
the available evidence does not yet distinguish them.

Openness does not remove obligations. Each later route requires its own
question, affected entities, evidence and acquisition basis, identity and
provenance contract, uncertainty representation, access/retention policy,
domain review, misuse analysis and human authority boundary. A later research
profile may refine this Ethics note; it must not silently bypass the
Observer/Controller separation or convert uncertainty into a target label.

Specialized defensive, security or forensic questions require a separately
reviewed legal and ethical basis. Their existence does not generalize into
permission for covert behavioral profiling or repurpose current method results
for surveillance, coercion or targeting.

# KSODI Open Research Questions v3.51

This public research companion records questions that remain intentionally
open after the v3.51 method and reference review. It does not replace the
versioned method files, alter a formula, create an implementation requirement
or make every question release-blocking.

Read it together with
[KSODI Research Status and Open Questions v3.51](./KSODI-Research-Status-and-Open-Questions_V351.md),
which classifies the currently defined method, common mathematical forms,
KSODI-specific choices, empirical gaps, alternatives and non-claims.

The governing separation is:

```text
accepted v3.51 boundary
!= open research question
!= candidate route
!= future method decision
```

The accepted basis is complete typed `Z` with attributable operator evidence;
distinguishable `IK_w`, `IK_M2`, `IK_ref` and `AxisDev_ref` readings; a
navigable **IK-Gesamtbild** / `ik_joint_evaluation_view` rather than one total
score; exact profile-bound `R_0` eligibility; current primary dyadic `IK_rel`
over one shared canonical `IK_w` profile; non-operative rounded displays; and
windows that retain opportunities, members, typed exclusions, denominators,
coverage, profiles and provenance.

## OR-V351-01 — Gate-excluded but potentially meaningful movement

When does an investigation question survive a closed `R_0` gate, and under
which declared conditions does a later gate transition become worth studying?

Accepted boundary: preserve both complete monadic trajectories, `Delta Z`, the
gate value/state and the exclusion reason; do not fabricate current `IK_rel`.
Canonical `R_0` is not calculated from IK. A gate transition is not by itself
convergence, relation, learning, therapeutic effect or causality.

Candidate route: separately named exclusion diagnostics or the human-readable
working label **IK-konditionierte R0-Übergangsanalyse** / English
**IK-conditioned R0 transition analysis**, with technical identifier
`ik_conditioned_r0_transition_analysis`, left-censored histories, alternative
trajectory paths and independent outcome evidence.

## OR-V351-02 — Positive epsilon semantics

Which positive values, if any, are justified for
`epsilon_numeric_tolerance`, `epsilon_observation_resolution` or
`epsilon_question_relevance` in a declared question and instrument setting?

Accepted boundary: exact zero remains the transparent reference. The three
epsilon kinds have different status consequences, and v3.51 defines no
universal positive default. A positive value records origin/evidence and
calibration status; declaration alone is not calibration.

Candidate route: instrument- and question-specific calibration, boundary
fixtures and comparison of false exclusions, false inclusions and unresolved
observations.

## OR-V351-03 — Extended IK_rel component profiles

Which fixed component sets beyond primary `{move}` have a defensible meaning
for a declared question?

Accepted boundary: each profile fixes a nonempty `N`, binds weights to
component names and prohibits event-wise removal or renormalization. Structural
evaluability does not establish empirical usefulness.

Candidate route: question-to-component matrices, counterexamples and separate
validation for every proposed profile.

## OR-V351-04 — Mixed window status and evidence sufficiency

How should large or dense windows report mixtures of numeric, closed-gate,
`not_observable`, `not_applicable` and processing records?

Accepted boundary: `strict_integrity_v351` retains the full partition;
uncontained processing failure blocks a valid aggregate; all-unobservable
evidence may yield `not_observable`; otherwise failed count or coverage yields
typed insufficient evidence rather than zero.

Candidate route: adversarial mixture fixtures and a later
`partition_preserving_window_aggregation` profile with bounded processing
tolerance (`PPWA-bounded`). This is not a v3.51 empirical default.

## OR-V351-05 — Minimum count, coverage and R0 threshold profiles

Which minimum numeric count, `coverage_rel` and
`theta_R0_gate_open_min` support which investigation question without tuning
the method toward a desired result?

Accepted boundary: v3.51 defines no universal empirical values. Store exact
numerators, denominators, profiles, exclusions, parameter origin/evidence and
calibration status.

Candidate route: predeclared profiles, sensitivity analyses and joint review
of gate-open/exclusion curves with complete trajectory evidence.

## OR-V351-06 — Question-adequate diagnostic bundles

Which smallest evidence bundle supports a human or machine investigation
without turning one projection into a total explanation?

Accepted boundary: current `IK_rel`, raw paired `Delta IK_w`, complete paired
`Delta Z`, directed componentwise differences and only separately selected,
defined and valid later diagnostics remain distinguishable. The optional
Phase-H coordinate-extreme candidate does not change `R_0` or replace complete
signed movement.

Candidate route: extend the question–measurement–view matrix and test false
inferences as well as useful distinctions. Decide separately in v3.52 whether
the coordinate-extreme candidate enters a controlled record schema, remains
test-only or is rejected.

## OR-V351-07 — Comparable point-cloud and corridor views

How should point clouds, distributions and reference corridors be compared
without losing membership, multiplicity, exclusions, profile identity or the
return path to individual evidence?

Accepted boundary: profile/status partitions and exact members remain visible;
excluded observations are not numeric zero.

Candidate route: explicitly named metrics and strata, sensitivity analysis,
privacy review and reverse-navigable Hangar views.

## OR-V351-08 — Event validity between observations

When may an event-derived state be treated as valid between events in a truly
time-weighted analysis?

Accepted boundary: current clock-time event means do not silently carry states
forward or interpolate them.

Candidate route: a separately validated state-validity profile with gap,
uncertainty, stopping and missingness rules.

## OR-V351-09 — Assistive LLM interpretation

Which bounded evidence package permits an assistive LLM to explain KSODI
results without letting interpretation overwrite measurement authority?

Accepted boundary: deterministic calculation, assistive interpretation and
human/governance view remain separately attributable consumer projections.

Candidate route: versioned semantic evidence packages, omission and
adversarial tests, disclosure of unavailable evidence and an explicit human
review path.

## OR-V351-10 — Change at different object levels

How should event-level `Delta`/`Delta2`, differences between window summaries
and point-cloud/distribution drift be displayed and interpreted together?

Accepted boundary: these are different derived objects with different members,
signs and comparability contracts.

Candidate route: question-led derivation names and visibly distinct v3.52
Basic Visuals with drill-down to operands, profiles, statuses and evidence.

  and authority; no such content is specified by this v3.51 companion.

Publishing an open question records a reproducible research boundary. Closure
requires an attributable evidence basis, valid scope, version placement,
implementation/publication consequence and reopening condition. Under
`Phi schließt nie`, such closure remains bounded rather than absolute.

These questions disclose research boundaries and possible investigation
routes. They do not promise implementation, establish binding priority among
future versions, validate a hypothesis, authorize deployment or invite
Observer results to control action. Contributions are welcome when they name
the question, evidence basis, profile, scope, uncertainty, privacy implications
and the accepted v3.51 boundary they preserve or challenge.
